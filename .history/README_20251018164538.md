#📍 Braj Museum – Virtual Gallery of Mathura Temple Heritage

🌐 Live Site: https://braj-museum.netlify.app

Braj Museum is a digital heritage experience that brings together the sacred and artistic legacy of Mathura through an immersive web-based gallery. This interactive platform showcases:

🛕 Temples of Braj & Mathura

🗺️ Interactive Map of heritage sites

📸 Photos & Videos from key locations

🎥 3D Video Walkthroughs of temple architecture and sculptures

🧭 Navigation between the museum’s collection and real-world temple sites

✨ Features

Mapped Gallery View: Explore an interactive map showing major temples and artefact locations across the Braj region.

3D Visuals & Video Integration: View 3D video presentations of temple exteriors, interiors, and museum artefacts.

Temple–Museum Linkage: Discover how sculptures and inscriptions from ancient temples are preserved in the Mathura Museum.

Responsive Design: Fully accessible on desktop and mobile devices.

🧱 Tech Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Leaflet.js for interactive mapping

Embedded 3D/video content (via iframe or external tools like Sketchfab/YouTube)

Hosted on Netlify

🎯 Purpose

This project aims to digitally preserve and promote the cultural heritage of Braj, especially Mathura's unique temple history and the artefacts housed in the Government Museum, Mathura. It serves as a tool for:

Students & researchers of art history or archaeology

Devotees and spiritual travelers interested in Krishna's Braj

Virtual tourists exploring Indian heritage online
